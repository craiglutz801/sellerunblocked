# SellerUnblocked Landing Page Architecture & Design Doc — V2

**Purpose:** Update the SellerUnblocked marketing landing page to introduce **IC Enablement Kits** as a core product breakthrough, not a secondary feature.

**Audience for this doc:** Cursor / engineering / product builder

**Primary goal:** Make the landing page communicate that SellerUnblocked is not only a leadership intelligence platform. It is a closed-loop revenue organization system that turns seller feedback into two outcomes:

1. **Leadership intelligence** — aggregated diagnostics, themes, benchmarks, and system-level recommendations.
2. **Seller enablement** — private, personalized kits that help individual sellers improve immediately.

---

## 1. Strategic framing

### Current landing page story

The current landing page positions SellerUnblocked as a **Revenue Organization Intelligence** platform for CROs, RevOps, Sales Ops, Enablement, and GTM Strategy leaders. The current flow tells a strong executive story:

- Revenue teams measure outcomes but not the operating environment producing them.
- Hidden friction hurts productivity, retention, and execution.
- SellerUnblocked diagnoses the seller environment through structured insight, analytics, benchmarking, and AI recommendations.
- The product is not another survey tool; it is a system for diagnosing and improving sales org performance.

This is good and should remain the foundation.

### New strategic opportunity

The IC Enablement Kits create a much stronger category story:

> **SellerUnblocked does not just tell leaders what is broken. It helps every seller act on what they personally need.**

This moves SellerUnblocked from:

> survey → dashboard → leadership action

To:

> survey → organizational intelligence **plus** private seller enablement

That is the sharper wedge. Most tools stop at dashboards. SellerUnblocked closes the loop.

### Updated positioning

**SellerUnblocked is the Revenue Organization Intelligence platform that diagnoses hidden sales friction and turns seller feedback into immediate leadership action and personalized enablement for every rep.**

### Updated one-line pitch

**Find what is slowing sellers down. Fix the system. Help every seller improve now.**

### Updated category language

Keep the category: **Revenue Organization Intelligence**.

But evolve the supporting definition:

> Revenue Organization Intelligence is the operating layer that measures whether the sales organization is helping sellers win — and turns those signals into both executive decisions and individualized seller support.

---

## 2. Core narrative architecture

The landing page should tell this story in four acts.

### Act 1 — The blind spot

Revenue leaders measure pipeline, forecast, win rate, quota attainment, and deal velocity. Those are outcome metrics. They do not reveal whether the sales organization itself is helping or hurting sellers.

### Act 2 — The hidden cost

Operational friction shows up as wasted selling time, inconsistent execution, poor enablement adoption, slower deal cycles, seller frustration, and preventable attrition. By the time it appears in revenue metrics, damage is already underway.

### Act 3 — The old feedback loop is incomplete

Most organizations either:

- collect feedback and produce dashboards for leadership, or
- push generic enablement content to the field.

Neither closes the loop well.

The rep who raised the issue often gets no useful help. Leadership may see themes, but the individual seller experiences feedback as a one-way vent.

### Act 4 — SellerUnblocked closes the loop

SellerUnblocked uses one structured diagnostic spine to produce two valuable outcomes:

- **For leadership:** aggregated intelligence, trends, benchmarks, friction themes, and recommendations.
- **For IC sellers:** private, AI-curated enablement kits based on their own feedback, scores, stage confidence, and context.

This is the breakthrough.

## 4. Global design direction

### Design principle

The landing page should feel like a premium B2B SaaS category-creation page:

- clean
- modern
- executive
- slightly futuristic
- credible
- not playful
- not HR-ish
- not generic survey-tool style

### Visual hierarchy

Use the existing landing page design system as the source of truth. Do not introduce a second visual language for IC Kits. The new IC Kits content should feel native to the current brand.

### Recommended visual metaphors

Use these motifs throughout the new sections:

1. **Diagnostic spine** — one structured data source branching into leadership and IC experiences.
2. **Closed loop** — feedback becomes action, not just reporting.
3. **Two outcomes** — system-level fixes and seller-level support.
4. **Private kit** — a polished, personal card stack that feels useful and safe.

### Avoid

- HR performance-review vibes
- generic LMS/course catalog visuals
- “manager coaching scorecard” language
- individual seller ranking language
- punitive red/yellow/green performance labels
- overly cute illustrations

## 28. Design specifications

### Layout

Use a consistent landing page container:

- max-width: 1120px–1200px
- center aligned
- generous vertical spacing
- mobile-first responsive layout

### Cards

Use existing card styling from the landing page. If needed:

- border radius: 20px–28px
- subtle border
- soft shadow
- white or slightly tinted background
- hover lift for interactive cards

### Color

Reuse the current landing page palette. Do not introduce a jarring new color family.

Recommended semantic usage:

- Primary brand color for CTAs and key highlights
- Soft neutral backgrounds for strategic sections
- Subtle gradient background for the IC Kits mockup
- Green or positive accent only for progress / completion states
- Avoid aggressive red for seller gaps; use neutral “gap” language instead

### Typography

Use current typography scale.

Recommended hierarchy:

- Hero headline: largest display style
- Section headline: strong, executive, concise
- Subheadline: 18–22px equivalent
- Body: readable, spacious
- Card labels: small uppercase or semibold metadata

### Motion

Keep motion tasteful.

Recommended:

- Fade in sections on scroll
- Slight hover lift on cards
- Optional subtle animated branch line in dual-outcome diagrams
- Optional progress animation in kit mockup

Avoid:

- excessive bouncing
- gamified animations
- LMS-style progress confetti
