# SellerUnblocked — Landing Page Design & Content Document
## Cursor-Ready Spec for a High-Conversion Website

## 1. Strategic Recommendation

The strongest version of this landing page should **not** position SellerUnblocked as a survey tool.

It should position SellerUnblocked as a new category:

# **Revenue Organization Intelligence**
### with seller experience as the input layer

That is the most compelling synthesis across the source documents.

This framing is stronger because it:
- sounds strategic, not tactical
- elevates the buyer from enablement-only to CRO / RevOps / GTM leadership
- makes the product feel like an operating system for revenue performance
- supports a larger category narrative
- preserves seller feedback as the mechanism, but not the headline identity

## Recommended core positioning

### Primary category
**Revenue Organization Intelligence**

### Secondary framing options
- **Sales Org Health Platform**
- **Revenue Intelligence for the Seller Experience**
- **The system for measuring whether your sales organization is actually set up to win**

## Best positioning statement
**SellerUnblocked helps revenue leaders measure the health of their sales organization by revealing the operational friction, misalignment, and support gaps that slow sellers down.**

---

## 2. Website Goal

The landing page should do one primary job:

### convince a CRO, RevOps leader, or GTM executive to book a demo

Secondarily, it should:
- make the category feel obvious and urgent
- explain why current revenue metrics are insufficient
- establish SellerUnblocked as the first credible solution
- reframe seller feedback as strategic revenue intelligence
- make the product feel premium, modern, and category-defining

---

## 3. Target Buyer

## Primary audience
- Chief Revenue Officers
- Revenue Operations leaders
- Sales Operations leaders
- GTM Strategy leaders
- Sales Enablement leaders at larger organizations

## Ideal company profile
- B2B companies with 100+ sellers
- scaling or operationally complex sales orgs
- multiple systems, teams, motions, territories, and layers of management
- organizations experiencing rep productivity, retention, or execution issues

## Buyer pain
They already track:
- pipeline
- win rates
- forecast accuracy
- quota attainment
- deal velocity

But they **cannot clearly see the internal operating environment** that produces those outcomes.

---

## 4. Messaging Strategy

## The narrative arc
The page should follow this sequence:

1. **Open with the big idea**  
   Revenue leaders are blind to the condition of the sales organization itself.

2. **Name the problem clearly**  
   Outcomes are measured. The environment producing them is not.

3. **Show the consequences**  
   Friction lowers productivity, slows deals, increases turnover, and hurts revenue.

4. **Introduce the category**  
   SellerUnblocked is a Revenue Organization Intelligence platform.

5. **Explain how it works**  
   Structured seller insight → diagnostics → analytics → AI recommendations.

6. **Show what gets measured**  
   process, tools, enablement, lead quality, compensation, support, alignment, etc.

7. **Tie everything to business outcomes**  
   higher productivity, stronger retention, better decisions, better revenue performance.

8. **Close with authority and action**  
   If you measure customer experience, you should measure seller experience.

# Section 3 — The Category Insight

## Goal
Make the market blind spot feel obvious.

## Headline
# **Revenue Teams Measure Outcomes. Almost None Measure the Environment Producing Them.**

## Body copy
Revenue leaders track pipeline, forecast accuracy, win rates, quota attainment, and deal velocity.

But those metrics only show the result.

They do not show whether the sales organization itself is structurally helping sellers succeed—or quietly making success harder.

That means the biggest operational barriers often stay hidden:
- sales process friction
- tool inefficiencies
- enablement gaps
- marketing misalignment
- unclear compensation
- internal support breakdowns
- territory or coverage imbalance

By the time these issues show up in revenue metrics, the damage is already underway.

- Quota

- Misalignment
- Enablement quality
- Support effectiveness
- Organizational health

# Section 6 — How It Works

## Goal
Show a simple intelligence loop.

## Headline
# **A Continuous Intelligence Loop for Revenue Organizations**

## Step 1 — Gather Seller Insight
Structured programs collect quantitative and qualitative feedback from sellers about the systems, processes, and support structures that affect performance.

## Step 2 — Diagnose the Organization
SellerUnblocked identifies patterns, friction points, operational gaps, and areas of misalignment across the revenue organization.

## Step 3 — Deliver Leadership Intelligence
Executives receive dashboards, summaries, benchmark views, and AI-guided insight into what is helping—or preventing—sellers from winning.

## Step 4 — Improve and Track Progress
Leaders make targeted operational improvements and measure whether the seller experience improves over time.
